The route

I first sat in a kart at eight. By 2017 it had turned into a professional career: karting, then ice racing, then a steady climb through the Russian circuit ladder. The next step is Asia.

  1. 2017

    Where the professional career started. Karting: rental racing first, then competitive events and the Sodi World Series — the foundation of racecraft and car control.

  2. 2021–2022

    Russian Ice Racing Championship, RAF Academy class. Won the title. Ice taught me confidence at the limit.

  3. 2023

    RCRS, S1600. The Russian Circuit Racing Series is the top circuit racing championship in Russia — I made my debut there at fifteen, with a podium in my first full season.

  4. 2024

    RCRS, Touring Light with RUMOS Racing. Two race wins.

  5. 2025

    RCRS Endurance, Touring. Podium at the opening round and a second place later in the season.

  6. 2026

    A transition year: first laps in GT4 machinery, testing in a Mercedes-AMG GT4, international opportunities and putting together a GT programme in Asia.

  7. 2027

    China GT, GT4 class. A full season, and the start of a long-term international programme.

  8. Next

    GT3, with GT World Challenge Asia as one of the main long-term targets.
